# Detailed human demonstration assets

The anatomical base mesh, adult male morph, muscle morph, skeleton/weights, high-poly eyes and brown-eye texture are graphical assets from MakeHuman Community. The skin, short01 hair, eyebrow001, male_casualsuit04 garment geometry and shoes05 meshes/textures are from the official MakeHuman system-assets CC0 pack.

These graphical assets were released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. Attribution is retained voluntarily: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier and MakeHuman Community contributors. Asset licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Official pack: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html . Upstream: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman . `sources.json` records pinned URLs, ZIP member offsets and SHA-256 hashes.

No MakeHuman application code is copied or linked; its application-code licence differs from the CC0 graphical-asset licence. The conversion script, runtime renderer, sports palette and animation mapping in Breda Fit are original project code. Source garment logos/textures are not used.

The displayed character is an adult fictional model, fully clothed. It is not a biometric scan, medical simulation or substitute for professional instruction. All assets are packaged inside the APK; the app downloads no model or user information at runtime. Movement illustrations require professional review before commercial publication.
